SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 2026-03-01
by and between Vertex Consulting LLC ("Party A") and Meridian Retail Inc.
("Party B").

WHEREAS Party A provides software consulting services, and Party B wishes
to engage Party A for such services, the parties hereby agree as follows:

1. Party A shall deliver monthly infrastructure audits.
2. Party A shall provide a dedicated support engineer during business hours.
3. Party B shall pay Party A within 30 days of invoice.

This Agreement is effective as of 2026-03-01 and expires on 2027-03-01.
Governing Law: State of New York.
